* CPU Information

Since I will be benchmarking primarily on one system, it is important to capture the relevant info about my system.
Here’s what I’ve gleaned

ISA: x86_64
CPU: AMD Ryzen 7500F
Cores/threads: 6 physical cores, 2 threads per -> 12 threads
Caches:
- L1d :: 192 KiB - 6 (each core has its own)
- L1i :: 192 KiB - 6
- L2 :: 6 MiB    - 6
- L3 :: 32 MiB   - 1 (shared)

Non-NUMA (this means non-uniform-memory-accesss). AMD uses something called infinity fabric, meaning traffic is not routed through one FSB but rather all cores have equivalent distance and latency to the memory controller.
